brb.scenario

Rehearse the moment.
Before it matters.

Scenario brings AI security to life through guided simulations and tabletop exercises. Your team works through a realistic situation, makes decisions together and discovers what needs to change.

A rehearsal for the decisions ahead

A tabletop is a guided conversation around an unfolding incident. No live systems are disrupted. The pressure comes from the questions, the choices and what happens next.

Scenario puts your people inside that story. New information arrives. Assumptions are challenged. Leaders, IT and the people using AI work out how they would respond, before a real incident forces the issue.

The aim is simple: understand your exposure, test your response and leave with practical improvements.

It started with a faster way to work

An everyday decision
becomes a security question.

A colleague uses an AI assistant to summarise client contracts. Then a customer asks where their information has gone. What do you stop, what do you need to find out, and who takes responsibility?

  1. Set the scene.

    Start with the tools, people and information that matter to your business.

  2. Work through the story.

    As events unfold, decide what to do. Explore who has authority, what is missing and where your plans hold up.

  3. Turn decisions into action.

    Capture the gaps, agree priorities and assign owners.

  4. Rehearse again.

    Use the findings to improve your controls, then test that they work.
