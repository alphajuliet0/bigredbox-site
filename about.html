About

Still curious.
Always hands‑on.

bigredbox is founder-led. Ajay Panchal, technologist, security leader and entrepreneur, leads every engagement.

Why bigredbox

Good security starts with understanding how things work. bigredbox exists to turn that understanding into clear decisions and practical progress.

A career built
from the ground up.

From the server room.

Ajay started in academia, working hands-on with networks, firewalls and large Linux clusters. Keeping those systems running taught him to understand the detail and the people who depend on it.

To the boardroom.

That foundation took him into Big Four consultancy and security leadership across central government, critical national infrastructure and policing. His work has spanned operations, architecture, risk and assurance, including CAF and GovAssure.

Still building. Still asking questions.

A founder, entrepreneur and technology enthusiast, Ajay enjoys making things work and helping people understand what to do next. bigredbox brings that practical mindset to businesses that need experienced support without unnecessary complexity.

Today, that includes AI: what it can access, which decisions it can make and who remains accountable. Through brb.scenario, we help teams explore those questions, find the gaps and take action.
