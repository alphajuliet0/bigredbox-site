04

Red teaming

Attackers start with people. So do we.

An objective-led simulation of a determined attacker. Most real attacks start with a person, not a firewall, so the human side is front and centre: convincing phishing, calls to your helpdesk, pretexting and, where agreed, physical access to your premises. People, process and technology are tested together, under rules of engagement agreed with you in advance.

  • Social engineering · phishing, phone calls and pretexts built around how your organisation really works
  • Process under pressure · password and MFA resets, payment approvals and the checks that should stop them
  • Physical access · reception, tailgating and site access, only where agreed in writing
  • Agreed objectives · what a real attacker would be after, and what is off limits
  • Controlled engagement · a named contact on your side and clear stop conditions
  • Detection and response · what your team saw, when, and what they did about it
  • Debrief · the attack path explained, focused on process rather than blaming individuals, and the changes that would break it
You leave withA clear account of what an attacker could reach through your people and your systems, and the fixes and training that matter most.
